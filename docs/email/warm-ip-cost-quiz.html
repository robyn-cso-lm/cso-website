Seven quick questions. About two minutes.

I grew up in this field. My mom, Joanne, started Canadian Surrogacy Options in 1992, and I’ve been around surrogacy since I was 4 years old. In all that time, one thing has never changed: families deserve the real numbers before they decide anything.

The questionnaire asks about Canada or the U.S., where you live, your timing, whether you’ll need an egg donor (through our sister company, Little Miracles), and what worries you most. Then it shows you an honest range for your situation.

Thinking about the U.S.? There’s a version for that too: see the Camica U.S. estimate.
