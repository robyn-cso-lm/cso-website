## 1. Federal law (all pages)
- Paying a surrogate a fee is prohibited under the Assisted Human Reproduction Act; documented expenses may be reimbursed. Minimum age 21 for a surrogate.
- Selling eggs or sperm is prohibited; donors may be reimbursed for receipted expenses.
- Sex selection is prohibited except to prevent a serious sex-linked disorder.
- Advertising that offers or asks for payment for surrogacy is prohibited.

## 3. Other statements to confirm
- International page: baby born in Canada is generally a Canadian citizen; citizenship for the baby in the intended parents' country depends on that country; DNA testing may be required; plan time in Canada after birth.
- FAQ "Can a surrogate keep the baby?": wording is that it is very unusual and that provincial processes are built so intended parents become legal parents.
- Benefits FAQ: surrogates who qualify can receive EI maternity benefits; eligible Canadian intended parents can receive EI parental benefits; Quebec uses QPIP.
