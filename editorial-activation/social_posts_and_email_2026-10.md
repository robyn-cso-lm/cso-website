## Video 1: Are surrogates paid in Canada?
**Hook text:** Are surrogates paid in Canada?

"Here's the question I get every week. Are surrogates paid in Canada? The honest answer is no. It's illegal to pay a surrogate a fee here. But she is reimbursed for real, receipted expenses: medical costs, travel, maternity clothes, childcare, and in some cases lost income. Over a whole journey that's usually about twenty-five to thirty-five thousand dollars, held in escrow so everything is transparent. I grew up in this field, since I was four years old, so ask me anything."

**Caption:** Are surrogates paid in Canada? The honest answer. From hope to heartbeat to home. 💜 Full guide: link in bio.

## Video 2: Can a surrogate keep the baby?
**Hook text:** Can a surrogate keep the baby?

"This is the fear I hear most from intended parents. In Canada, almost all surrogacy is gestational, which means the surrogate has no genetic link to the baby. The agreement and the provincial parentage process are built so that you become the legal parents. Is it ever possible she changes her mind? I'll never tell you never. But careful screening, counselling and a good match make it very unusual. Ask me how we protect families."

**Caption:** The question every intended parent is afraid to ask. 💜

## Video 7: Can I choose the sex of my baby?
**Hook text:** Can you choose your baby's sex in Canada?

"Short answer: no. Choosing the sex of an embryo for non-medical reasons is prohibited under the Assisted Human Reproduction Act. The only exception is to prevent a serious sex-linked genetic disorder. I'd rather you hear it from me now than find out halfway through."

## Video 8: Is egg donation safe? (LM)
**Hook text:** I was an egg donor five times. Here's the honest truth.

"Egg donation involves hormone injections and a short procedure under sedation. Serious complications are uncommon. A reaction called OHSS affects about one percent of our donors, and everyone is monitored closely. If you have rapid weight gain, severe bloating or trouble breathing, call your clinic right away. I'll always give you the whole picture, risks included."

**Caption:** Thinking about donating? Ask me anything. 💜

**Hook text:** Living outside Canada? You can still do surrogacy here.
