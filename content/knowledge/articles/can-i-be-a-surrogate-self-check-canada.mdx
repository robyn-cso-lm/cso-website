If you are thinking about becoming a surrogate, I want to start with this: thank you. Wanting to carry a baby for a family who cannot is one of the most generous things a person can do.

Here is a friendly self-check to help you see whether you might be a fit. It is not a pass or fail test. Final decisions are made after medical and psychological screening, so if you are unsure about anything, please ask.

## Age

- Are you at least 21? That is the legal minimum in Canada.
- Most agencies and clinics prefer candidates up to about 40 to 45, and older candidates receive extra medical review.

## Pregnancy history

- Have you had at least one healthy pregnancy and birth? This is the most common requirement.
- Without a prior pregnancy, some candidates can still be considered with extra review.
- Have you had a C-section? Not automatically a no. It is reviewed case by case.

## Health

- Are you generally healthy, with a body weight within the range a fertility clinic requires?
- Do you have conditions that are managed or under control?
- Please be honest with us. Many things that sound disqualifying are reviewed case by case.

## Lifestyle

- Do you smoke or vape? Smoking is not an automatic no at CSO, but it must stop during pregnancy. We ask about your willingness to quit.
- Do you use recreational drugs? These are not compatible with surrogacy.

## Health coverage

- Do you have a provincial health card? Not having one is a flag we look at, but it is not an automatic disqualifier.

## Life circumstances

- Is your life stable right now, with housing and a support network?
- If you have a partner, are they supportive?
- Can you commit to 12 to 18 months of appointments, medications and travel?

## Your reasons

- Are you doing this because you want to help a family?
- Surrogates cannot be paid a fee in Canada. They are reimbursed for expenses.

## What happens if you check most of these boxes

1. **Application** to us
2. **Medical review** and screening at a fertility clinic
3. **Psychological evaluation** with a counsellor
4. **Matching** with intended parents
5. **Independent legal advice** and a surrogacy agreement
6. **Embryo transfer** and pregnancy
7. **Birth and aftercare**, including emotional support

You will have a support person on our team at every step.
