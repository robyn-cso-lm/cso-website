No. Under the Assisted Human Reproduction Act, choosing the sex of an embryo for non-medical reasons is prohibited in Canada. The exception is to prevent a serious sex-linked genetic disorder.

This is one of the questions I am asked often by families coming from countries where it is allowed. It is better to know before you begin.
