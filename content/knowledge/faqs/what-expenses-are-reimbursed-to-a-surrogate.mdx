Typical reimbursable items include prenatal and medical costs not covered by insurance, travel to appointments, maternity clothing, childcare during appointments, medications and counselling. Lost income can be reimbursed in limited cases supported by a physician.

Everything must be tied to the pregnancy, and every claim needs a receipt. Your agreement lists what is covered.

Read more: [Surrogacy Reimbursements in Canada](/knowledge-centre/surrogacy-reimbursements-canada-guide).
