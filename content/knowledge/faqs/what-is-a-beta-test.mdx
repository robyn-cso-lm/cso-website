A beta test is a blood test that measures hCG, the pregnancy hormone, after an embryo transfer. Clinics usually do the first one about one to two weeks after transfer, then repeat it a couple of days later to see whether the number is rising the way an early pregnancy should.

A few things I tell every family and surrogate:

- **One number is not the whole story.** The trend between the first and second beta matters more than any single result.
- **Home tests can mislead.** Trigger shots and early timing can cause false readings, so the blood test is the one that counts.
- **Keep taking meds** until the clinic tells you otherwise, whatever the result.
- **Decide ahead of time** how the surrogate will share the news with the intended parents. It makes beta day much gentler for everyone.

Your clinic will tell you what your numbers mean for you.
