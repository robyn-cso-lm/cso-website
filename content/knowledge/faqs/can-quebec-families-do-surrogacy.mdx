Yes, but Quebec has its own rules. Since 2023 the Civil Code has allowed surrogacy projects with a notarized agreement signed before the pregnancy begins, an information session for the parties and specific consent steps after the birth.

Rules differ depending on where the surrogate lives, and out-of-province surrogacy involves extra steps. Please work with a Quebec notary or lawyer alongside your agency.
