Ontario funds one IVF cycle for eligible patients under 43, though fertility drugs are not included. Quebec and British Columbia also offer funding, and Manitoba, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island and Newfoundland and Labrador offer tax credits, grants or reimbursements.

Programs, limits and eligibility change often. Check your own province's official program and ask your clinic what applies to you before you budget.
