In most situations the intended parents are named as the legal parents, but the exact process depends on the province where the baby is born. Some provinces use a written pre-conception agreement and consent after birth, while others use a court order.

Your lawyer will tell you which steps apply where your surrogate lives and delivers. We plan for this at the very start of every journey so there are no surprises at the hospital.
