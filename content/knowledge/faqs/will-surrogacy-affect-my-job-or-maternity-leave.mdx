A surrogate pregnancy is a pregnancy, so pregnancy leave protections generally apply. Surrogates who qualify can receive EI maternity benefits, and eligible Canadian intended parents can receive EI parental benefits. In Quebec, the Quebec Parental Insurance Plan applies instead.

Talk to your employer early so there are no surprises. Benefit rules are changing, so confirm the current details on [Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/ei/ei-maternity-parental.html) before you plan.
