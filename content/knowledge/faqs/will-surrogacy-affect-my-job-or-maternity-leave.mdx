A surrogate pregnancy is a pregnancy, and employment protections for pregnancy leave generally apply. Surrogates may be eligible for maternity benefits, but check your province's rules and Service Canada for your situation.

Talk to your employer and your lawyer early so there are no surprises. Our team can help you plan around it.
