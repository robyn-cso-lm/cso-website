Camica agency programs are $28,000 (Signature), $38,000 (Priority) and $48,000 (Premier) USD, with Camica Concierge from $65,000 by application. Surrogate compensation, medical, legal and escrow costs are separate.

Surrogate compensation in the US typically runs from about $55,000 to $70,000 or more, so the total is much higher than the agency fee alone. We will give you a full estimate on a call.
