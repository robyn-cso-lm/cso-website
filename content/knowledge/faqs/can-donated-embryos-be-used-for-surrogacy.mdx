Yes. A gestational surrogate can carry a donated embryo in Canada, as long as the embryo donors consented to that use and everyone has proper legal agreements in place. It is a real path for people who need help with eggs, sperm and carrying.

A few things to check early:

- **Consent:** the donors' consent forms need to allow the embryos to be used by you, with a surrogate. Your clinic will review this.
- **Legal agreements:** you will have an embryo donation agreement with the donors and a separate surrogacy agreement with your surrogate, each with independent legal advice.
- **Shipping:** if the embryos are stored at another clinic, they may need to be transported to the clinic doing the transfer.
- **Parentage:** your provincial rules decide how you become the legal parents, and your lawyer will walk you through it.

Your baby will not share genes with you or your surrogate, and that is perfectly okay. Love is what makes a family.
