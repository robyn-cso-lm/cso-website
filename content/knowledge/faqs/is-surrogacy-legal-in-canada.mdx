Yes. Surrogacy is legal in Canada, but only in an altruistic form under the Assisted Human Reproduction Act. A surrogate cannot be paid a fee, though her documented pregnancy-related expenses can be reimbursed.

Federal law sets the ground rules, and each province handles legal parentage in its own way. That is why a lawyer who works in reproductive law, in your province, matters so much.
