A complete gestational surrogacy journey in Canada typically runs between $80,000 and $120,000 CAD for intended parents, covering agency fees, surrogate reimbursements, legal, clinic and insurance costs.

Where you land in that range depends on your clinic, the number of transfers, your surrogate's circumstances and how smoothly things go. Our cost article walks through every line item.
