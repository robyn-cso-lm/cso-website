Yes, if she wants to and the intended parents want it too. Pumping and sending breast milk is the most common arrangement, while direct breastfeeding is less common. It is entirely the surrogate's choice, and the plan should be agreed well before the birth.

Good conversations to have early:

- **How long** she is willing to pump, and whether she can stop any time (she can).
- **Logistics:** pump, bottles, storage bags, freezing and how milk gets to the family.
- **Expenses:** which pumping supplies and costs will be reimbursed with receipts.
- **Her recovery:** pumping takes time and energy during the fourth trimester.
- **If she does not want to:** her milk will come in anyway, and she deserves support to dry up comfortably.

Some intended mothers also explore inducing lactation with their doctor. Every option is a loving one.
