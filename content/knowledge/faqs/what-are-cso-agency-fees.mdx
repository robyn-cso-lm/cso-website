CSO programs start from $1,500 for a self-guided Independent Journey, $9,500 for Foundation, $19,500 for Guided Journey and $29,500 for Priority Match (CAD, plus HST). The agency fee is paid in three equal stages, never all upfront.

These are 'from' prices, and what you pay depends on the program that fits you. Agency fees are only one part of the total budget. Surrogate reimbursements, legal, clinic and insurance costs are separate.
