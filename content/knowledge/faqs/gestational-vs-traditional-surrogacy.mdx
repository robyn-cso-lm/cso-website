In gestational surrogacy the baby is created through IVF and the surrogate has no genetic link to the child. In traditional surrogacy the surrogate's own egg is used. Gestational surrogacy is the standard in Canada today.

Traditional surrogacy is rarely supported today because of the added legal and emotional complexity. Most agencies, clinics and lawyers in Canada work with gestational surrogacy only.
