No. Paying a surrogate a fee is illegal in Canada. She can be reimbursed for real, receipted pregnancy-related expenses, such as medical costs, travel, maternity clothing and, in some cases, lost income when a doctor advises she stop working.

Reimbursements are paid from funds the intended parents place in escrow, and every claim is backed by a receipt. This protects the surrogate, the intended parents and the baby.

Read more: [Surrogacy Reimbursements in Canada](/knowledge-centre/surrogacy-reimbursements-canada-guide).
