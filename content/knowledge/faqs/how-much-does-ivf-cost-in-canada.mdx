It varies by clinic, by province and by what your cycle needs. Very approximately, a privately paid IVF cycle with medications often lands somewhere around $15,000 to $25,000 or more. Some provinces fund part of treatment, so always get a written quote from your own clinic.

When you compare quotes, check what is included:

- Monitoring, egg retrieval, lab work and the embryo transfer
- **Medications,** which are often billed separately and can be a big piece
- Extras like ICSI, embryo freezing, storage and PGT-A testing
- Any frozen embryo transfers after the first cycle

If you are using a surrogate, add surrogate screening and her transfer cycle. Private insurance plans sometimes cover fertility medications, so check your benefits too.
