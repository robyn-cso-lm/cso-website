The Assisted Human Reproduction Act (AHRA) is the federal law, passed in 2004, that sets the ground rules for surrogacy and donation in Canada. It makes it a crime to pay a surrogate or to buy or sell eggs, sperm or embryos, while allowing receipted reimbursement of expenses under federal regulations.

The big pieces, in plain words:

- **No payment** to surrogates or donors. Reimbursement of eligible, receipted expenses is allowed.
- **No buying or selling** of eggs, sperm or embryos.
- **A surrogate must be at least 21.**
- **Consent** is required for any use of donated material.
- **Sex selection** is banned, except to prevent a sex-linked disorder.

Breaking these rules is a federal criminal offence. The AHRA does not decide who the legal parents are. That is provincial law, which is why your lawyer matters.
