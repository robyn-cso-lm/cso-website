It is rare, and I will not pretend it is impossible. A surrogate always keeps the right to make decisions about her own body, and legal parentage is usually only confirmed after the birth. Careful screening, counselling and a good match are what make this very unlikely.

Honest conversations before matching, a strong agreement, and support from the first day to the last are your best protection. If you want to talk through this fear, it is one I hear a lot and I am happy to be straight with you about it.
