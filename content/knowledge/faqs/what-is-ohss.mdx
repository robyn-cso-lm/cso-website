OHSS is ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome, a reaction to fertility medications that can cause swelling, bloating and, rarely, more serious symptoms. It occurs in roughly 1% of Little Miracles donors, and monitoring helps catch it early.

Tell your clinic right away about rapid weight gain, severe bloating or trouble breathing. Do not wait it out.
