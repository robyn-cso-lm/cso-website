Yes. Egg freezing is legal and offered at fertility clinics across Canada, both for medical reasons like cancer treatment and by personal choice. Elective freezing is usually paid privately, while some provinces help with fertility preservation before medical treatment.

The process looks a lot like the first half of IVF: about two weeks of hormone injections and monitoring, then a short egg retrieval under sedation. The eggs are frozen and stored until you are ready.

Honest things to know:

- **Age matters.** Eggs frozen at a younger age generally work better. Your clinic will talk to you about your own odds.
- **It is not a guarantee.** It improves your options, it does not promise a baby.
- **Storage fees** continue every year.
- **Your eggs stay yours.** Freezing your own eggs for your own use is very different from selling eggs, which the law bans.

As someone who donated eggs five times, I can tell you the process is very doable with good support.
