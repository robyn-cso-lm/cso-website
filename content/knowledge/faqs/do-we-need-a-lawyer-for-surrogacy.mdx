Yes. Intended parents and the surrogate each need their own independent lawyer experienced in reproductive law, and the surrogacy agreement should be signed before any medical procedures begin.

Two separate lawyers is not a formality. It protects everyone and keeps the agreement strong if anything is ever questioned later.
