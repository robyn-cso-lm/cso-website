In gestational surrogacy the surrogate has no genetic link to the baby, and the agreement and the provincial parentage process are built so that the intended parents become the legal parents. A surrogate who changed her mind after the birth would be very unusual.

The details of how and when parentage is confirmed vary by province, which is exactly why each side has its own lawyer.
