Some of them, possibly. Canada's Medical Expense Tax Credit can cover eligible fertility and surrogacy related medical expenses, including certain medical costs reimbursed to a surrogate. Agency fees and most legal fees generally do not qualify. Keep every receipt and have an accountant confirm what you can claim.

What tends to help:

- **Keep clean records.** Escrow statements and receipts make it much easier to sort medical costs from everything else.
- **Look at clinic and medication costs,** which are the most common eligible items.
- **Ask about timing.** The credit is based on a 12-month period, and choosing it well can make a difference.
- **Check provincial credits.** Some provinces, including Quebec, have their own fertility related tax measures.

I am not a tax professional, and the rules are specific, so please get advice from an accountant who knows CRA rules.
