Yes. Intended parents can usually claim Employment Insurance parental benefits to care for their newborn, as long as they meet the normal eligibility rules. Maternity benefits are tied to pregnancy and birth, so they generally belong to the surrogate. Quebec parents use the Quebec Parental Insurance Plan instead.

A few practical tips:

- **Two kinds of benefits:** EI benefits (the money) are federal, while job-protected leave comes from your provincial employment standards or federal labour code. Check both.
- **Standard or extended:** EI parental benefits can be taken over a standard or an extended period, and can be shared between two parents.
- **Talk to HR early.** Many employers have never handled a surrogacy birth, and some offer top-ups.
- **Rules change.** The federal government has discussed extra weeks for adoptive and surrogacy families, so check canada.ca for the current rules before you apply.
