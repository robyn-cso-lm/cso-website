It varies a lot by bank, by type of donor and by how many vials you need, so get current quotes from your clinic and bank. The vials are only one piece: shipping, storage, clinic fees and the treatment itself (IUI or IVF) usually add up to more than the sperm.

When you build your budget, ask about:

- **Price per vial**, which often differs for open-identity donors and for vials prepared for IUI versus IVF
- **How many vials** your clinic suggests, especially if you might want a sibling from the same donor later
- **Shipping** to your clinic, which is often a separate charge
- **Storage fees** if vials sit at the clinic for a while
- **Exchange rates**, since many banks price in US dollars
- **The treatment itself**, which is usually the biggest cost

A known donor avoids bank fees but still involves screening, counselling and a lawyer.
