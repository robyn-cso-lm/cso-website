An agency screens and recruits surrogates, helps match intended parents and surrogates, coordinates clinic and legal steps, and supports everyone through the journey from first call to birth and beyond.

Different agencies include different things, so read the contract carefully. At CSO, we also guide families through the stages that catch people off guard.
