Yes. MRKH means being born without a uterus (or with an underdeveloped one), but the ovaries usually work. That means many people with MRKH can do IVF with their own eggs and have a gestational surrogate carry their genetic child.

What the path usually looks like:

1. **A fertility clinic consult** to check your ovarian reserve and plan egg retrieval. Your doctor will confirm the best approach for your anatomy.
2. **IVF** to create embryos with your eggs and your partner's or a donor's sperm.
3. **Matching** with a gestational surrogate.
4. **Embryo transfer** to your surrogate, and the pregnancy from there.

If you learned about MRKH as a teenager, you may have carried this for a long time. Please know you are not alone, and the grief is real. To grieve is to know love. A biological family is still very possible.
