Smoking is not an automatic rejection at CSO, but you cannot smoke during a surrogate pregnancy. We ask about your willingness to quit and what support you would need.

We are not here to judge. We are here to see if the timing is right for you and for the family.

Read more: [Surrogacy Requirements in Canada](/knowledge-centre/surrogacy-requirements-canada).
