The surrogate's pregnancy and delivery care are generally covered by her provincial health plan like any other pregnancy. IVF and medications for the intended parents are mostly paid out of pocket, though some provinces offer limited fertility funding.

Funding programs change, so check your own province's current rules and ask your clinic what is covered for your situation.
