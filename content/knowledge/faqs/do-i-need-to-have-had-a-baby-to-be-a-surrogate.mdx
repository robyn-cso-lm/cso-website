A previous healthy pregnancy and birth is the most common requirement, and many clinics strongly prefer it. In some cases women without a prior pregnancy can be considered, with extra screening and the right clinic fit.

A prior pregnancy shows your body can carry to term, and it means you understand what pregnancy and birth feel like before doing it for someone else.

Read more: [Becoming a Surrogate in Canada](/knowledge-centre/becoming-a-surrogate-in-canada).
