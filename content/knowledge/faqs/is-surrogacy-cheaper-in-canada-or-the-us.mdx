Canada is usually much cheaper. A typical Canadian journey runs about $80,000 to $120,000 CAD all in, because surrogates can only be reimbursed for expenses and provincial health plans generally cover pregnancy and delivery. In the US, surrogate compensation and private medical costs push budgets well higher.

But cost is not the only difference:

- **Wait times:** Canada has fewer surrogates than families looking, so matching can take longer.
- **Law:** Canada is altruistic only. Many US states allow compensated surrogacy and pre-birth parentage orders.
- **Healthcare:** in the US, you need a plan for the surrogate's medical insurance and hospital bills.
