Yes. A sister, cousin or other relative can be a surrogate or donor in Canada. The same rules apply as for anyone else: medical screening, counselling, independent legal advice for each person, and no payment beyond receipted expenses. Clinics will not allow combinations that would be genetically too close.

Family journeys can be beautiful. They also need extra care:

- **Counselling is a must.** It helps everyone talk about roles, boundaries and how you will explain things to the child.
- **Genetics:** for example, a woman's brother usually cannot provide sperm to be combined with her eggs. Your clinic will confirm what is allowed.
- **Pressure:** a relative should never feel obligated. Saying no has to be safe.
- **Legal steps:** you still need agreements and a parentage plan, even within a family.
