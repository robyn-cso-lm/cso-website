Yes, you can get donor sperm through a licensed sperm bank or fertility clinic in Canada. What the Assisted Human Reproduction Act bans is paying a donor for his sperm. Banks charge for screening, processing, storage and shipping, and much of the donor sperm used in Canada is imported from banks outside the country.

Here is the honest picture:

- Donor sperm has to meet Health Canada's safety rules, including donor screening and testing. Your clinic will only accept sperm from a source that meets them.
- Because Canadian donors cannot be paid, there are few Canadian donors, so many families choose from imported donor catalogues.
- Using a known donor (a friend or relative) is also an option, with its own screening, counselling and legal steps.

Your clinic and your lawyer will confirm what applies to your situation.
