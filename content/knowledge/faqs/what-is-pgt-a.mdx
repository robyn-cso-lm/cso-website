PGT-A stands for preimplantation genetic testing for aneuploidy. A few cells are taken from an IVF embryo, usually at the blastocyst stage, and checked for the right number of chromosomes. It can help choose which embryo to transfer, but it is not perfect and it is not right for everyone.

What families should know:

- **It screens chromosome number,** not every genetic condition. Testing for a specific inherited condition is a different test.
- **Results can be unclear.** Some embryos come back "mosaic," meaning a mix of normal and abnormal cells, and your clinic will explain the options.
- **It costs extra** and is usually not covered by provincial funding.
- **Experts still debate** who benefits most. Your fertility doctor will help you decide based on your age, history and number of embryos.

In surrogacy, many families like the extra information because each transfer involves another person's body and time. Talk it through with your doctor.
