A donor-conceived person is someone who was conceived using donated eggs, sperm or embryos. Many donor-conceived people say knowing their story from an early age, and having some access to information about their donor, matters a lot to them.

What I share with every family using a donor:

- **Tell early and often.** Children who grow up knowing their story tend to take it in stride. Finding out later, especially from a DNA test, can feel like a betrayal.
- **Keep what you can.** Save the donor profile, photos and any letters for your child.
- **Think about siblings.** One donor may help several families, so your child could have donor siblings.
- **Their feelings are theirs.** Curiosity about a donor is not rejection of you.

I donated eggs five times, so I hold this from both sides. Openness is a gift to everyone.
