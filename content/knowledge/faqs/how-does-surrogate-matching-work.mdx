We share intended parent profiles with screened surrogates and surrogate profiles with intended parents. When both sides feel a connection, they meet, ask questions and agree to move forward.

A match has to be mutual. Shared values on communication, medical decisions and involvement during the pregnancy matter more than anything on paper.
