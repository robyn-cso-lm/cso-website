Yes. There are no legal restrictions in Canada based on marital status. Single intended parents can pursue surrogacy, using their own gametes or donor gametes as needed.

Many of the families I have worked with have been single parents by choice. Your agency and lawyer will make sure the paperwork reflects your family structure from day one.
