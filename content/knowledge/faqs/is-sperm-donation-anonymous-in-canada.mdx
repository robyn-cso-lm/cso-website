It can be, but it is becoming less so. Anonymous donation is still generally allowed in Canada, many banks also offer open-identity donors who agree to be contactable once the child is an adult, and consumer DNA tests mean no donor can truly be promised anonymity anymore.

There are usually three kinds of donor:

- **Anonymous:** you see a detailed profile, but no identifying information.
- **Open identity (ID release):** the donor agrees his identity can be shared with the child at adulthood.
- **Known:** someone you already know, like a friend or relative.

Rules on a child's right to know their origins differ by province and keep changing, so ask your clinic and lawyer what applies where you live.

My radically honest advice: choose as if your child may one day find their donor, because they very well might. Many families now choose open identity for exactly that reason.
