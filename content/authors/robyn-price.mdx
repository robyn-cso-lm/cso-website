Robyn Price is the Executive Director of Canadian Surrogacy Options (CSO), Canada's first surrogacy agency, founded in 1992 by her mother, Joanne Wright. Robyn has been part of the surrogacy world since she was 4 years old and has led CSO since about 2014.

In 2007 she founded Little Miracles, an egg donation company, and she has been an egg donor herself five times. She also operates Camica, CSO's US-facing surrogacy program.

She writes and reviews resources for intended parents, surrogates, egg donors and families exploring surrogacy in Canada. Her approach is simple: honest answers about process, timelines and costs, with real support at every step.

*From hope to heartbeat to home.*
